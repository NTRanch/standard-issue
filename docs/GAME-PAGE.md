# Standard Issue

## 18+ — Adults Only

This game contains adult content. It is intended only for players 18 and older.

### Content notes

- An adult exam game: earn one Certified Standard across two papers, Fit and Signals.
- Every paper opens with teaching — safety first, always shown and never gated; the test is the teach.
- Each paper is opened by speaking its phrase, and a pass is entered in the book of record.
- Tier arithmetic is stated in the book: the second tier needs Fit and Signals together, and the third needs two Certified Standards — this book holds one, and says so.

All characters depicted are adults.

## What you do

Every paper opens with teaching: safety first, always shown, never gated. Then comes the paper's plug — press B to speak its phrase (Fit: *MEASURE ME.* — Signals: *READ ME.*) and the exam opens. In Fit, hold the canvas or the space bar to raise the marker and release to let it settle, keeping it in the sweeping zone; a second in the zone is a rep. In Signals, a four-glyph signal flashes and is taken away, and you reproduce it on the glyph pad, by heart.

Pass, and the standard is entered in the book of record — the moment it is earned, aloud. Three exams build on each other: the Fit Standard alone, the Signals Standard alone, and then both papers in one sitting, with more reps required.

## Features

- Three exams: 8 reps inside 60 seconds for Fit, 4 of 5 signals whole for Signals, and both papers in one sitting — 10 reps and 4 of 5 signals — for the final gate.
- A teaching layer in every paper, with safety basics never gated.
- Settings for exam pace and text size, and the book of record, persist in the browser, with settings reachable before any play.
